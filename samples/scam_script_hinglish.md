# Role-play script: "digital arrest" scammer (FOR DEMO ONLY)

> Read aloud by a teammate playing the scammer, on speakerphone, near the laptop/phone running Kavach.
> Pause about 2 seconds between lines so each ~5 s audio chunk carries roughly one line.
> These lines are taken from publicly reported digital-arrest scripts, so judges can recognise the pattern.

1. **(Authority)** "Hello, namaste. Main Mumbai CBI cyber cell se Inspector Vikram Singh bol raha hoon."
2. **(Accusation)** "Aapke Aadhaar card se ek FedEx parcel book hua hai jismein drugs aur paanch fake passport mile hain."
3. **(Threat)** "Aapke naam par money laundering ka FIR aur arrest warrant nikal chuka hai."
4. **(Secrecy)** "Ye case bahut confidential hai. Kisi ko mat batana, apne bete ko bhi nahi."
5. **(Isolation)** "Abhi video call par aaiye. Aap digital arrest mein hain, call mat kaatna."
6. **(Money)** "Apni innocence prove karne ke liye RBI verification ke liye saare paise safe account mein transfer kariye."
7. **(Urgency)** "Do ghante mein payment nahi hua to local police aapke ghar aa jayegi."
8. **(OTP)** "Jo OTP aaya hai woh abhi bataiye, paisa 24 ghante mein refund ho jayega."

**Expected:** instant flags from line 1. The AI score crosses 70 by about line 4 to 5, and Bulbul speaks the warning.
